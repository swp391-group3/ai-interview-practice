The principles

Less performance. More practice.

DATN is designed to make clear thinking visible, shared, and more durable.

We begin with what matters, what must remain human, and what the work is meant to change. Production follows the decision.

Methods become more valuable when they are named, shared, and improved in contact with other perspectives.

We use new technology to increase the range and reach of judgment, never to flatten it into a default.

A thoughtful artifact can create a vocabulary others can use. We make space for that life beyond the project itself.
